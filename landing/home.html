No more context-switching between tools

Cryptography

Hash files, compute HMACs, inspect JWTs, and verify signatures locally without pasting secrets into random web tools.

Regex Toolkit

Test patterns, catch risky expressions, check portability, and keep reusable regexes close to your shell.

Network & URLs

Check status codes, inspect headers, measure DNS/TCP/TLS/TTFB timings, and break URLs into readable parts.

Certificate Ops

Generate local X.509 certificates, inspect remote TLS chains, and spot expiry problems before they become deploy problems.

Data Utilities

Generate UUIDs, passwords, strings, integers, bytes, and Base64 output with predictable flags and copy-friendly results.

Format & Time

Extract JSON fields, compare payloads, clean sensitive keys, and convert timestamps without switching tools.
